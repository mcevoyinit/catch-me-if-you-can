# ArbiLens: The Regulatory Navigation Protocol ⚖️
*ETH Global NYC Hackathon Project*

**Protocol enabling transparent, compliant navigation of global regulatory frameworks.**

## 🌍 Empowering Global Citizens & Entrepreneurs

A transparent protocol that democratizes access to regulatory navigation, helping individuals and small businesses compete on a level playing field with large corporations in the global economy.

## 📊 Project Summary

**ArbiLens** is a Regulatory Navigation Protocol that helps businesses and individuals make informed, compliant decisions when operating across jurisdictions. Rather than leaving regulatory understanding to expensive legal teams at large corporations, this protocol makes sophisticated compliance analysis accessible to everyone.

### Core Innovation
The protocol calculates Expected Value (EV) for regulatory strategies by analyzing:
- Legitimate business opportunities across jurisdictions
- Compliance costs and requirements
- Innovation sandbox and special economic zone opportunities
- International business structure optimization

### The Formula
```
EV = ProfitPotential - ComplianceCosts - RegulatoryRisks - OpportunityCost - TimeDelayCost
```

- **Work Permit Arbitrage**: Find the best jurisdictions for remote work

### For Small Businesses
- **Regulatory Sandboxes**: Identify innovation-friendly jurisdictions
- **Market Entry Analysis**: Evaluate new market opportunities with clear risk assessment
- **Compliance Optimization**: Find the most efficient path to regulatory compliance

### For Startups
- **International Expansion**: Data-driven decisions on which markets to enter
- **Product Launch Strategies**: Navigate gray areas in emerging tech regulations

### For Individuals
- **Career Mobility**: Understanding visa and work opportunities globally
- **Investment Strategies**: Navigate international investment regulations
- **Education Arbitrage**: Finding the best educational opportunities globally

## 🔍 How It Works

1. **Strategy Input**: User proposes a business strategy or opportunity
2. **AI Analysis**: LLMs analyze the legal context and regulatory landscape
3. **Risk Calculation**: Real-world enforcement data feeds into EV calculation
4. **Transparent Output**: Risk score with clear recommendation (EXECUTE/CAUTION/AVOID)
5. **On-Chain Attestation**: All calculations are cryptographically sealed and logged

## 🌟 Key Benefits

- **Transparency**: All decisions are auditable and traceable

The goal is to democratize access to regulatory intelligence, helping small players understand compliance requirements and opportunities that large corporations already navigate with dedicated legal teams.

## 🚀 Technical Architecture

- **Core Engine**: Python-based EV calculation system
- **Data Oracles**: Integration with legal databases and enforcement statistics
- **Attestation Layer**: TEE simulation for cryptographic proof
- **Blockchain Integration**: Immutable logging of all analyses
- **API Layer**: RESTful endpoints for easy integration
